About Our Department

Discover the vision and academic environment of MCA.

Department Overview

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) programme focuses on developing advanced knowledge in computer science, software development, programming and information technology.


The department aims to encourage technical excellence, innovation, research and industry-oriented learning among students.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision: To develop competent professionals capable of contributing to the evolving world of computing.


Mission: To promote quality education, practical knowledge, research activities and ethical professional development.
